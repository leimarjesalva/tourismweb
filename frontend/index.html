Discover Legazpi

Legazpi City, the capital of Albay province in the Bicol Region of the Philippines, is a vibrant destination known for its breathtaking natural wonders, rich cultural heritage, and warm hospitality. It is most famous for the majestic Mayon Volcano, often hailed as the world’s most perfectly shaped cone.
